## Reading context

A useful fieldnote explains the observation, the surrounding context, and the limits of the evidence. <span id="prose-word">Understanding</span> comes from those connections, not from fitting every sentence onto one line.

> [!NOTE] Keep the context
> <span id="callout-word">Understanding</span> needs room alongside the evidence. This note is supplementary; the main conclusion stays in the prose.
>
> - Keep the original source.
> - Explain the uncertainty.
>   - Record the <span id="list-word">observation</span> before drawing a conclusion.

> A quoted explanation should remain readable without consuming the whole screen in indentation.

1. Read the source and the surrounding passage.
2. Follow [the next section](#follow-the-evidence).
3. Revisit the conclusion when the evidence changes.
