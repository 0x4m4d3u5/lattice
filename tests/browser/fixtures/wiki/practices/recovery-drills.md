## Before the exercise

Choose a synthetic snapshot, confirm the destination is isolated, and assign one person to run the exercise while another records the observations.

## Check the result

Compare totals and a sample of records with the manifest. Record what surprised you and which steps needed more explanation.

## Leave a record

Use [[review-checklist]] to make the record useful for another reader. This example uses fictional data and describes no live service.
