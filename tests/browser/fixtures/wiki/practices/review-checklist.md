## Read for meaning

Can a new reader tell what the note is for? Is the next action clear? Remove repeated background and link to the source of the detail.

## Follow the links

Open each related page and check heading targets. Return through the backlinks to see whether the relationship makes sense in both directions.

## Check the small screen

Read the note on a narrow viewport. Check that navigation is reachable, keyboard focus is visible, and paragraphs stay comfortable to read.
